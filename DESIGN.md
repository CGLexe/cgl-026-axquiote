## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, contemplative, and tactile culinary experience celebrating contemporary Oaxacan gastronomy rooted in native terroir and heritage techniques. The visual atmosphere is calm, intentional, and literary—reminiscent of independent art monographs, tactile linen paper, sun-dried clay (*barro*), and the quiet majesty of the Central Valleys of Oaxaca.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Warm Editorial Minimalism:** Massive structural breathing room, strict visual editing, and gallery-like restraint. Avoid decorative flourishes; let negative space frame culinary narratives.
- **Organic Tactility:** Surface interactions evoke raw linen, adobe, and natural stone textures through quiet warm neutrals and delicate hairline divisions.
- **Architectural Poise:** Asymmetrical balance, stately classical typography paired with modern airy sans-serifs, and deliberate layout pacing mirroring an omakase-style seasonal tasting menu.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the Oaxacan kitchen and earth: burnt clay (*barro rojo*), wild mountain herbs (*hierba santa* and deep sage), native golden maize, and woodfire charcoal.

### Palette Implementation
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base substrate rests on natural linen (`#FAF7F2`), layering into warmer vessel tones (`#F4EFE6`) for card containers, drawer sheets, and subtle section divisions. Stark digital white (`#FFFFFF`) is strictly avoided.
- **Primary (`#A44A2D` - Barro Rojo / Terracotta):** Reserved for singular focal points, primary reservation actions, date selections, and curated seasonal highlights.
- **Secondary (`#505A43` - Salvia Profunda / Deep Muted Olive):** Applied to badges, culinary category tags, botanical notes, cellar curation, and secondary status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C98A3C` - Maíz Dorado / Warm Ochre):** Used sparingly for sensory accents, vintage notations, tasting menu chapter indicators, and subtle celebratory badges.
- **Typography & Structural Neutrals:** Deep espresso charcoal (`#1F1D1A`) provides high-legibility contrast without the synthetic harshness of pure black. Secondary captions, metadata, and dish provenance use soft clay charcoal (`#3A352F` and `#686056`).
- **Hairlines & Dividers:** Subtle adobe wash (`#E6DFD3`) serves as the foundational border color across all containers.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a serene, literary dialogue between classic Oaxacan historical gravitas and contemporary modernist legibility.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines (EB Garamond):** Set with generous line heights and balanced tracking. Dish names, seasonal essays, philosophy statements, and Chef notes must appear in EB Garamond to preserve an artisanal, tactile print presence.
- **Body & Editorial Copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with open aperture, generous letterforms, and low weight contrast to ensure fatigue-free reading across ingredient descriptions, provenance details, and reservation itineraries.
- **Labels, Metadata, & Nutritional Details:** Always rendered uppercase in Plus Jakarta Sans with elevated letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to function as crisp catalog markers against the natural linen background.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to an editorial grid prioritizing rhythmic white space and unhurried horizontal balance.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid bounded inside a `1440px` maximum viewport container. Margins expand to `4rem` (or `6rem` for dedicated reading vistas), allowing images of culinary creations and pottery to breathe without lateral tension.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Menu cards reflow into 2-column arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Elements flow in a structured vertical scroll reminiscent of a pocket tasting booklet.

### Architectural Rhythms
- Spacing steps `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` must be utilized between thematic sections (e.g., between "Filosofía del Fuego" and the Tasting Menu tiers) to reinforce an atmosphere of quiet luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain faithful to natural materials, elevation relies on delicate tonal layering and razor-sharp hairline borders rather than artificial drop shadows.

### Depth Principles
- **No Drop Shadows:** Heavy blur shadows and diffuse dark drops are strictly banned to avoid an overtly synthetic or software-like appearance.
- **Linen-on-Sand Layering:** Depth is established through subtle color steps: the base canvas sits at `#FAF7F2`, while elevated surface cards, reservation modules, and modal drawers shift to `#F4EFE6`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Delimit stacked tiers and interactive containers using a continuous, quiet 1px outline of `#E6DFD3`.
- **Atmospheric Modal Sheets:** Overlays and flyout wine lists utilize a backdrop wash of `#1F1D1A` at 30% opacity paired with a soft `4px` blur, maintaining focus while keeping the warm ambient background subtly visible.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a strict architectural profile with crisp, unrounded corners (`0px`), recalling handcrafted paper cuts, stone-cut mezcal stills, and bespoke menu printing.

### Rules of Form
- **Cards, Frames, and Modals:** Rendered with sharp `0px` corners. Visual softness is introduced through spacious internal padding and warm organic coloration rather than corner radii.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Clean, squared forms that emphasize structure and precision.
- **Culinary Photography & Media:** Framed in unrounded, gallery-standard aspect ratios (3:4 portrait or 16:9 landscape) flanked by clean hairline borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid terracotta (`#A44A2D`) background with ivory linen text (`#FAF7F2`), sharp corners, uppercase `label-lg` typography, and generous horizontal padding (`space-xl` horizontal, `space-md` vertical). Subtle hover state shifts to a rich roasted tone (`#8E3E24`).
- **Secondary (Outline):** Transparent fill enclosed by a 1px border of `#1F1D1A`. Text in `#1F1D1A`. Hover transitions background to `#F4EFE6`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost / Editorial Link):** No border, inline link styling paired with a hairline underline offset by 4px, accompanied by an understated directional glyph (`→`).

### Menu & Course Cards
- Background set in `#F4EFE6` enclosed by a 1px hairline border in `#E6DFD3`.
- Internal padding of `space-xl` on desktop (`space-lg` mobile).
- Dish titles formatted in EB Garamond (`headline-sm`), followed by a subtle horizontal rule and a concise list of native Oaxacan ingredients and pairing notes set in `body-sm`.

### Badges & Origin Chips
- Flat, rectangular pills with sharp corners (`0px`).
- Background: `#FAF7F2` bordered with a 1px outline in muted olive (`#505A43`) or terracotta (`#A44A2D`). Text styled in `label-sm` with matching tonal hue.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Flat input surfaces using `#F4EFE6` or transparent backgrounds bounded by a 1px bottom border in `#3A352F` (editorial ledger style) or a complete hairline frame in `#E6DFD3`.
- Placeholder text in muted clay (`#686056`). Focused state transitions the hairline to espresso (`#1F1D1A`) without glow rings.

### Tasting Menu Chapter Dividers
- Minimalist, full-bleed or column-bound section breaks composed of a centered Roman numeral or chapter title in EB Garamond (`headline-md`) framed by an expansive 1px hairline rule on both flanks.
